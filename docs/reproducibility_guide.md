# Reproduction guide

This is a provenance-focused **code-only** distribution, not a verified end-to-end pipeline. Follow `../RUN_ORDER.md` with original provider inputs and historically matched checkpoints in an isolated reconstruction tree; see `../metadata/source_manifest.tsv` and `../metadata/script_to_result_map.tsv`. Inputs and frozen output objects are **not bundled**. Where required, retain their original byte identity and pathway/date correspondence.

The two historical source gaps are documented in `source_gaps.md`. Historical software environments are listed in `../environment/`. All scientific commands and environment installations are **NOT TESTED** in this repository preparation. Static checks and SHA256 verification do not establish independent regeneration of manuscript figures or statistics.
