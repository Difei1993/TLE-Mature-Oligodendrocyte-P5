# Official data acquisition and expected inputs

No provider data were downloaded during staging. See metadata/datasets.tsv for official links and follow provider terms/citations. GEO supplementary RAW.tar filenames describe processed archive packaging; they do not imply that all raw sequence reads are available.

GSE190452 discovery input: gzip matrix.mtx/features.tsv/barcodes.tsv triples under `00_raw_data/GSE190452_human`, with the original GEO filename prefixes preserving sample_id/condition. The eight public GEO libraries are GSM5724021–GSM5724026, GSM6893724 and GSM6893725. Preserve the source's Normal/TLE coding; prose uses control donors. The missing clinical-metadata repair producer prevents claiming an independent complete raw-to-32B0 chain. Required checkpoint: `02_objects/human_harmony_r0p4_level1_annotated_with_clinical_metadata_repaired.rds`, with original RNA assay and per-nucleus library/donor/condition and Level1 annotation.

GSE140393 external input: retain the five source-selected TLE processed count samples and their GEO donor manifest, candidate/manual-annotation Seurat objects, and the separate official processed rld-normalized OLIG2-positive nuclear matrix. The source maps pediatric and adult donors explicitly; only the adult mature-OL comparison is the main between-dataset context. Do not convert normalized/rld data to claimed raw counts. Exact original filename patterns and Seurat output names are indexed by metadata/input_output_manifest.tsv and the original scripts.

SEA-AD input: matching MTG processed OL and OPC H5AD editions with the provider donor and subclass annotations. The extractor requires `raw.X` for counts; the R export validates nonnegative integer counts and symbol columns. Original inclusion constants are provider pseudonymous donor codes, not participant identity records, and remain untouched. Donor observation tables and H5AD subsets are excluded. The official current data distribution may differ from the archived edition; source hashes/edition matching remain a reproduction prerequisite.

GSE295237 input: GSM8944108 (Control_2), GSM8944109 (Control_3), GSM8944110 (FCDI_1), GSM8944111 (FCDI_2); matched matrices/features/barcodes and in-tissue Visium position tables. FCD I must not be relabeled TLE. Spot scores, patient metadata and whole expression matrices are excluded.

Regulator/glial prerequisites: original primary H5AD inputs, archived 34A1B2 score weights and latent prerequisite files, 34A1B5 consensus table, 34A2 donor program table, and dated CollecTRI full/complex-free core networks. Their original locations/hashes are available in archived evidence and source definitions; they are not reconstructed from current web resources.

NicheNet: obtain legally accessible human priors through the [official NicheNet repository](https://github.com/saeyslab/nichenetr), matching the frozen `lr_network_human_21122021.rds` and `ligand_target_matrix_nsga2r_final.rds`. Original scripts use priors stored in legacy STEP59/GSE264624 resource folders; these resource inputs do not justify including those projects' analysis scripts. Current downloads require hash comparison with the original provenance before they can be called the same resource.

Figures: 17 archived aggregate tables, two study-generated gene/weight checkpoints and three necessary SVG layers are represented byte-for-byte. Fifteen distinct SVG files plus three canonical aliases preserve 18 original logical locations. Two mixed-source signature catalogs are withheld pending rights; see [third-party input notices](third_party_notices.md) and `metadata/excluded_input_manifest.tsv`. Exact catalog-dependent redraw is not runnable from the bundled files alone. Data-driven redraw additionally requires frozen donor scores/usage, Figure4C archived residual coordinates, and spatial spot scores. Those individual observation tables are not bundled. The PDF incremental box-patch metadata is tied to original PDF bytes and is not supplied as a portable generic renderer. All re-execution remains NOT TESTED.

The included `34A1B2_PROGRAM_SCORE_GENES_AND_WEIGHTS.csv` is a study-generated cNMF program-weight table with a located producer. `34A5C_FROZEN_HUMAN_TOP100.csv` is a study-generated human P5 checkpoint; its independent historical exporter remains SOURCE NOT FOUND. These are not third-party regulator priors or raw human expression matrices. The two withheld catalogs are not replaced with new or approximately matched lists. Public provider accession/pseudonymous donor constants preserve input selection; they do not convey unrestricted permission to redistribute provider content.
